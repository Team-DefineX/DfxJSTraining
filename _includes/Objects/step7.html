In application code, objects are frequently nested; an object may have a property that is an array of further objects, which in turn may have a property that is another object!


We want an employees object in our car object. All employees who work for the company will be included in it as objects. They have properties like name, surname and level, and each has a special method based on their information. We can also nest other items in the car, like an object from the Administration Department.

Accessing nested properties is possible by chaining operators. Choosing the appropriate operator for each layer will require careful consideration. 

    
    car.employees.employee1.employeeInfo() // Returns 'Gökhan Ertaş as a Team Leader, manages the teams'
